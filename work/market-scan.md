## Size band
- **Segment counted:** informal-sector workers/vendors in Manila's flood-exposed barangays, current epidemic window.
- **Band:** tens of thousands — source: derived (see calculation) · 2026-09-13.
- **Calculation (glass-box):** Manila City population 1,846,513 (source: PSA 2020 Census of
  Population and Housing, City of Manila — https://rssoncr.psa.gov.ph/content/highlights-2020-census-population-and-housing-city-manila
  · ~2021) × informal-settlement share [assumption — no Manila-specific figure found; proxy is the
  NCR-wide 20–35% range reported in flood-vulnerability coverage, itself secondary] ≈ 370,000–650,000
  residents living in flood-exposed informal areas city-wide. The actively-exposed-this-epidemic
  subset is a small fraction of that: the city's own reported case count is 1,051 (source: Manila
  Bulletin, https://mb.com.ph/2026/09/08/manila-leptospirosis-cases-top-1000-amid-epidemic · 2026-09-08)
  as a visible floor, with the reachable "at risk this wave" population (anyone recently in
  floodwater in an affected barangay, not just confirmed cases) reasonably stated in the
  **tens-of-thousands** band per flood event — conservative relative to the full informal-settlement
  population, since not everyone in that population is exposed in any single flood event.

## Positioning evidence for §6
- "unlike the general information campaign" → the campaign raises awareness city-wide, not
  site-specific same-day guidance — EV-001, grounded in the mayor's directive.
- "because it repurposes rails that already exist" → the barangay SMS network already moves case
  data; extending it to move stock/hours guidance outward is not a new channel to build from zero — EV-008.
