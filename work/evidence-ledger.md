## A-001 coverage
- **A-001:** Informal workers/vendors in flood-exposed Manila barangays will act on a same-day,
  site-specific alert within their exposure window, at a meaningfully higher rate than they act on
  the city's existing general information campaign alone.
- **Evidence touching it:** none directly — EV-007 establishes the window exists and EV-001
  establishes the general campaign is the current baseline, but no evidence (primary or secondary)
  yet measures a differential response rate. **The MVP must test it** — this is exactly what
  `work/interview-guide.md` question 7 and any pilot alert would be the first evidence for.
