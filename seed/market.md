# Market seed — Manila Lepto Prophylaxis Access

## Size band
Tens of thousands (per epidemic wave) — source: PSA 2020 Census, City of Manila, 1,846,513 total
population (https://rssoncr.psa.gov.ph/content/highlights-2020-census-population-and-housing-city-manila,
~2021), narrowed by an [assumption] informal-settlement share (20–35%, NCR-wide proxy) and the city's
own reported case count of 1,051 (mb.com.ph, 2026-09-08) as a visible floor · calculation:
1,846,513 × 20–35% [assumption] ≈ 370,000–650,000 in flood-exposed informal areas city-wide;
actively-exposed-per-wave is a smaller, tens-of-thousands-scale subset of that.

## Where they gather (reachability)
1. 44 Manila city health centers + 7 district hospitals (the existing free-doxycycline distribution network) — verified 2026-09-13, source: pna.gov.ph/articles/1283462 (2026-09-07)
2. Barangay SMS rapid-reporting network (currently case-reporting only, re-purposable outbound) — verified 2026-09-13, source: pia.gov.ph "Beyond the flood" feature (undated within piece)

## Alternatives and their failure modes
1. City's general information campaign — fails at site-specific, same-day actionability (pna.gov.ph, 2026-09-07)
2. e-Konsulta / Bayanihan e-Konsulta teleconsultation — fails at being flood/lepto-specific and stock-aware; COVID-era general teleconsult tool (search synthesis, 2021 origin)
3. do nothing / wait for symptoms — fails at cost: delayed treatment ties to a documented 6.05% national CFR (philstar.com, 2026-09-04)
