# Brand seed — Agap Alerto

## Identity
- **Working name:** Agap Alerto *("agap" = timely/preventive action, "alerto" = alert; immutable
  technical IDs never change with a rename)*
- **One line:** For someone who just waded through floodwater in Manila, a same-day text or lookup
  telling them the nearest open, stocked place to get their free preventive dose — before the window
  closes.

## Tone
- **Is:** urgent-but-calm, plain-language, directive (tells you exactly where to go)
- **Is not:** clinical/authoritative-sounding (it is not a doctor), alarmist, bureaucratic

## Visual (if decided)
- **Colour:** undecided — a candidate direction is a high-contrast amber/red "act now" accent against
  a calm neutral base, legible on low-end screens and in bright outdoor light (barangay health center
  settings).
- **Type:** undecided — large, high-legibility body text is a hard requirement given outdoor/low-light
  use and a non-technical audience; specific typeface undecided.
- **References:** undecided — no reference products chosen this session.

## Banned copy (→ INV-### candidates and design-system banned-copy lines)
- The product must never say anything that reads as a dosing instruction or medical authorization
  (e.g., "take this now") — because DOH already warns against unsupervised self-medication with this
  exact drug (research brief R-010); the product's job is to route people to a supervised dose, not
  to prescribe.
- The product must never show a site as "open" or "stocked" past a short, stated freshness window —
  because a wasted trip during an active epidemic, to a population near hospitals already at
  capacity, actively harms the person it was meant to help.

## Accessibility / locale constraints
- Filipino/Tagalog and English, plain-language reading level (segment includes residents with
  varying literacy and no assumed smartphone fluency).
- Must degrade gracefully to SMS-only interaction — no requirement for a smartphone, app install, or
  mobile data plan (segment's device/data access is unconfirmed — see market.md assumptions).
